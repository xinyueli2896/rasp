Why B needs the auxiliary loss

In the integer experiment the equivalent projection fed a compiled TracR rule model whose frozen attention matrices did real computation, which pinned the projection to rule coordinates for free. CPChordRuleModel has no attention — it is a lookup — so nothing constrains ar_to_rule, and without a target the adapter collapses into ordinary self-attention over music features.

The auxiliary loss supplies that constraint explicitly: BCE on the triad chromagram plus CE on bar phase, against the analytic rule hidden for the window’s key. Targets are read from the dataset at training time only — inference still receives nothing. Setting PROXY_W=0 removes the loss and tests exactly this claim.
